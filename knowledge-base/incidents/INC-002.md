# Incident Summary
An operator bulk-replayed the payment DLQ without checking idempotency keys. Twelve duplicate downstream submissions were generated.

## Corrective Action
Bulk replay was prohibited and the runbook now requires individual transaction verification plus approval for batches above 20 messages.
